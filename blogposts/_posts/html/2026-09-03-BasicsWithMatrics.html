The calculation

The two arrows are the parallelogram's edges:

\(\mathbf a_1=(a_{11},a_{21})\) \(\mathbf a_2=(a_{12},a_{22})\)

Start from the full bounding box:

\(\text{box area}\) \(=(a_{11}+a_{12})(a_{21}+a_{22})\)

Subtract the colored pieces shown above:

\(\text{area}=(a_{11}+a_{12})(a_{21}+a_{22})\) \(\quad-\color{#c43b54}{\left(\tfrac12a_{11}a_{21}+\tfrac12a_{11}a_{21}\right)}\) \(\quad-\color{#147f7a}{\left(\tfrac12a_{12}a_{22}+\tfrac12a_{12}a_{22}\right)}\) \(\quad-\color{#b66a00}{a_{12}a_{21}}-\color{#7153b8}{a_{12}a_{21}}.\)

Expand the box, then cancel matching terms:

\(\text{area}=(a_{11}+a_{12})(a_{21}+a_{22})\) \(\quad-\color{#c43b54}{a_{11}a_{21}}-\color{#147f7a}{a_{12}a_{22}}\) \(\quad-\color{#b66a00}{a_{12}a_{21}}-\color{#7153b8}{a_{12}a_{21}}\) \(=a_{11}a_{21}+a_{11}a_{22}\) \(\quad+a_{12}a_{21}+a_{12}a_{22}\) \(\quad-\color{#c43b54}{a_{11}a_{21}}-\color{#147f7a}{a_{12}a_{22}}\) \(\quad-\color{#b66a00}{a_{12}a_{21}}-\color{#7153b8}{a_{12}a_{21}}\) \(\boxed{\text{area}=a_{11}a_{22}-a_{12}a_{21}}\)
